About the Salon

Beauty, Refined.

Glitz & Glam is a beauty salon in the heart of WAPDA Town, Lahore, built around one belief — that beauty should feel personal.

From professional makeup artistry and hair styling to indulgent facials and skin care, every service is tailored to you. Our experienced team prepares you for weddings, events and everyday moments in an environment designed to feel calm, elegant and entirely your own.

  • Personalized beauty experiences
  • Bridal & event preparation
  • Professional makeup, hair & skincare
  • An elegant, welcoming environment
