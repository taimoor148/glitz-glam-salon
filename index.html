Good to Know

Questions, Answered

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes — we are an appointment-only salon. Weekends and bridal dates fill quickly, so we recommend booking at least a few days ahead. Bridal packages are usually reserved 1–2 weeks before the event.

Do you offer bridal trials?

Absolutely. A trial lets us perfect your look before the big day — shades, placement and finish are agreed and documented so the final result is exactly what you loved.

What should I bring to my bridal consultation?

Photos or references you love, your outfit's swatch if you have one, and any products your skin prefers. If you have henna or an event back-to-back, tell us your timeline so we can plan it well.

Do you cater for parties and events?

Yes — party, walima and event looks are available for groups. Message us on WhatsApp for group bookings and on-location services.

How do I confirm or change my appointment?

Use the booking form above or message us on WhatsApp — we confirm every booking personally and are happy to move your date or time should plans change.
